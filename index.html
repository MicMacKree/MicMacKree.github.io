Gabrielle Uploader

A private upload tool for the channels of the animated series « Les aventures de Gabrielle ».

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Gabrielle Uploader is a small script that runs only on the personal computer of its author, Michael Le Thiec, an independent creator. It publishes the videos he produces (the animated series « Les aventures de Gabrielle » and its daily weather and good-news shorts) to the four YouTube channels he owns: French, German, Spanish and English versions.

What it does

  • When a finished video and its description file are placed in a local folder, it uploads the video to the right channel, with its title, description, tags, language and publication time.
  • It can set the video thumbnail and add the video to one of the channel's playlists.
  • It never downloads videos, never reads data about other users and never deletes anything.

Who can use it

Only its author, on his own channels. It is not offered to the public and is not a commercial service.
