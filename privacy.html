Who we are

Gabrielle Uploader is a personal tool operated by Michael Le Thiec (Allemant, France), contact miclet002@gmail.com. It is used only by its author, on his own YouTube channels.

Use of YouTube API Services

Gabrielle Uploader uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

With the authorization granted by the channel owner through Google sign-in (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube), the tool:

  • reads the name and identifier of the authorized channel, to check that videos go to the right channel;
  • creates videos, sets their thumbnails and adds them to playlists of that channel.

It does not access, collect or process any data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

Storage

The only data stored is the OAuth authorization (refresh token) for each channel, the channel name and identifier, and a local log of uploads (video file name, date, resulting video link). All of it is kept only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party.

Cookies and tracking

The tool sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. This website has no cookies either.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored authorization useless; the local files can then be deleted. Any request about this policy can be sent to miclet002@gmail.com.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
